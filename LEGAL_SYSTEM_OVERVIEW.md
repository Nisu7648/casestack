# ⚖️ LEGAL CASE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

**For Law Firms - Compete with MyCase & Clio**

---

## 🎯 WHAT IS THIS?

A **complete legal practice management system** for law firms where attorneys can:

1. **Search by client name** → Get ALL their cases, documents, court dates instantly
2. **Upload documents** → Auto-extract text, dates, parties, legal terms
3. **Track cases** → Court hearings, deadlines, billing, time tracking
4. **Manage clients** → Contact info, case history, billing
5. **Calendar** → Court dates, deadlines, meetings with reminders
6. **Time & Billing** → Track billable hours, generate invoices
