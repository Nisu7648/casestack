## 🎯 WHAT IS CASESTACK?

**CaseStack Enterprise** is a complete, production-ready consulting management system designed for internal adoption by Big-4 accounting firms (Deloitte, PwC, EY, KPMG) and top-tier consulting firms (McKinsey, BCG, Bain).

**This is NOT:**
- ❌ A productivity app
- ❌ A collaboration tool
- ❌ A project management system
- ❌ An AI-powered assistant
- ❌ A file storage solution

**This IS:**
- ✅ A system of record for consulting deliverables
- ✅ An audit-defensible compliance platform
- ✅ A structured report composition engine
- ✅ A long-term institutional memory system
- ✅ A partner-controlled workflow enforcer

## 🧩 COMPLETE SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

### **11 Fully Integrated Modules**

1. **Firm & Governance Layer** - Multi-tenant isolation, retention policies
2. **Identity, Roles & Control** - 5 strict roles, explicit permissions
3. **Immutable Activity & Audit Log** - Append-only, SHA-256 hashing
4. **Client Master & Historical Memory** - Complete engagement history
5. **Engagement & Report Lifecycle** - One draft per engagement (enforced)
6. **Structured Report Composition** - Versioned sections, no chaos
7. **Evidence & Reference Intelligence** - Metadata only, no file storage
8. **Review, Comment & Sign-Off System** - Threaded comments, resolution required
9. **Dossier & Professional Output Engine** - Professional PDF generation
10. **Enterprise Search & Retrieval** - Global search, advanced filters
11. **Data Retention & Long-Term Memory** - 10+ year retention, searchable archives

- ✅ IP address logging

### **Compliance**
- ✅ SOX compliance (audit trail)
- ✅ GDPR compliance (data export, soft delete)
- ✅ ISO 27001 compliance (security controls)
- ✅ 10+ year data retention
- ✅ Right to be forgotten (soft delete)
- ✅ Audit log export
- ✅ Regulatory reporting

### **Workflow Enforcement**
- ✅ One draft per engagement (database constraint)
- ✅ Finalized engagements read-only forever
- ✅ Unlock requires Admin + justification (50+ char)
- ✅ Resolution required before approval
- ✅ Partner-only final sign-off
- ✅ Comments cannot be deleted (only resolved)

### **Data Integrity**
- ✅ Immutable historical records
- ✅ Version tracking for sections
- ✅ State capture with SHA-256 hash
- ✅ Evidence checksum verification
- ✅ No file storage (references only)
- ✅ Frozen data after finalization
